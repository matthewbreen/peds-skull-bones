    How this model was made

    Labels were recovered from the axial review video of the Visage segmentation (one frame per 0.3 mm slice, registered to the source DICOM at r≈0.96). Each overlay pixel was classified by its legend color. Same-colored sub-parts (e.g. the three temporal parts) were separated using the 1-px outlines Visage draws between labels, then linked through z with a rule that never merges two pieces that were outlined apart on any slice.

    Surfaces: within ±0.35 mm of each traced contour the surface is pulled toward the CT bone edge (≈130 HU, appropriate for infant bone); this is switched off wherever two labels meet, so sutures and part boundaries stay exactly as traced. Light Gaussian/Taubin smoothing removes slice-to-slice jitter. Overview meshes are 0.38 mm; opening a section loads 0.20 mm meshes (0.11 mm for ossicles).

    Orientation: all preset views use the Frankfort horizontal (both porions and the left orbitale), with the midsagittal plane vertical. Relative to the scanner axial plane this head is pitched −2.4°, rolled −3.9° and yawed −7.2°. The slice views are resliced in the same frame (axial = FH plane, sagittal = midsagittal plane; switch to Acquisition planes to see the native slices).

    FH edges: the dataset's top and bottom are cut along the scanner axial plane, which is tilted to FH (6.6 mm across the calvarium, 4.7 mm across the mandibular rami). With FH edges on, the display is trimmed by two planes parallel to FH at the last level where both sides are complete (28.7 mm above and 18.7 mm below FH). Nothing is added and the segmentation is unchanged.

    Unlabelled right-side bones (ivory) are not your segmentation: they are the mirrored left label (about a CT-fitted midline plane) dilated 1.5–5 mm and intersected with CT bone. They are context only.
